# JuiceWeaver • UVR5 & MDX Ensemble Workflow Guide
**Evolving Documentation • v0.3**

## Overview
JuiceWeaver is the evolution of the StemWeaver pilot — rebranded for the juice (creative flow) it brings to music workflows.

## Evolved UVR5 Instructions (Inside the App)
The in-app guide and this MD evolve with each iteration. v0.3 adds expanded per-stem controls (Brightness, Warmth, Drive, Reverb Send) that give more interactive change in feel when used with the comprehensive demo stems (longer Vox with phrase contour, complex drum patterns with 16th variation, synth melody hooks, ground noise harmony strings, piano riffs with natural decay).
